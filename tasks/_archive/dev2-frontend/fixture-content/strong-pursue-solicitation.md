DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
Office of Acquisition and Logistics

REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Solicitation Number: 36C77624R0042

Title:               Cybersecurity Vulnerability Assessment Services
NAICS Code:          541512 — Computer Systems Design Services
Set-Aside:           Total Small Business Set-Aside
Place of Performance: Texas (regional VA facilities)
Period of Performance: 12-month base period plus two 12-month options
Proposal Due Date:    June 15, 2026, 4:00 PM Central Time
Questions Due:        May 28, 2026


SECTION B — SUPPLIES OR SERVICES AND PRICES

The contractor shall provide cybersecurity vulnerability assessment services
including but not limited to:

  - Network vulnerability scanning (internal and external)
  - Web application penetration testing
  - Configuration and policy review against NIST 800-53 controls
  - Remediation guidance and prioritized roadmap
  - Quarterly executive briefings to facility CIOs


SECTION L — INSTRUCTIONS TO OFFERORS

L.1  Proposal Format
     Volume I: Technical Approach (page limit: 25)
     Volume II: Past Performance (page limit: 10, see L.4)
     Volume III: Pricing (no page limit)
     Volume IV: Required Documents (see L.5)

L.2  Submission
     Proposals shall be submitted electronically via the agency portal no
     later than the date and time specified above.

L.3  Technical Approach
     Offerors shall describe their methodology for the listed assessment
     activities, tools used, and team composition. Specifically address
     experience with NIST 800-53 controls and FedRAMP-aligned environments.

L.4  Past Performance Narrative
     Offerors shall provide three references for similar cybersecurity
     assessment engagements completed within the last five years. Each
     reference shall include contract value, period, scope, and government
     point of contact.

L.5  Required Documents
     - SF 33 (signed)
     - Representations and Certifications (current)
     - Active SAM.gov registration (provide CAGE code)
     - Commercial general liability insurance certificate ($2M aggregate)

SECTION M — EVALUATION FACTORS FOR AWARD

The Government will use a best-value tradeoff source selection. The
following factors will be evaluated:

  Factor 1 — Technical Approach           60% weight
  Factor 2 — Past Performance             20% weight
  Factor 3 — Price                        20% weight

  Tradeoff: technical and past performance combined are more important
  than price. Award may be made to other than the lowest-priced offeror.


SECTION L (cont'd) — PERSONNEL AND CLEARANCE

L.6  Personnel
     Key personnel shall include a Project Manager, Lead Vulnerability
     Analyst, and Lead Penetration Tester. No security clearance is
     required for this contract; activities are limited to unclassified
     networks and systems.

L.7  Insurance
     The contractor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance
     of $2M aggregate limit during the period of performance.


SECTION H — SPECIAL PROVISIONS

H.1  Small Business Set-Aside
     This solicitation is set aside 100% for small business concerns under
     NAICS 541512 (size standard $34M average annual receipts).

H.2  Pricing Structure
     The contract shall be awarded as Firm-Fixed-Price for the base period
     and option periods, with quarterly invoicing.


SECTION I — CONTRACT CLAUSES

Standard FAR clauses apply. See Attachment 2 (clauses) for the complete
list. No amendments are anticipated; any changes will be issued via a
formal solicitation amendment.
