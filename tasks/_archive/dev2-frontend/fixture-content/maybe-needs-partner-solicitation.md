SECTION B — SCOPE OF WORK

The contractor shall design, build, and operate a 24/7 Security Operations
Center (SOC) supporting Treasury enterprise systems. SOC services include:

  - Tier 1, 2, and 3 incident response across all shifts
  - SIEM platform engineering and tuning (Splunk Enterprise Security)
  - Threat hunting and proactive analytics
  - Quarterly purple-team exercises
  - Continuous monitoring and reporting against NIST 800-53 controls
  - 24/7 staffing — minimum 14 cleared personnel rotating across shifts

     Volume I: Technical Approach (50 pages)
     Volume II: Management Plan (15 pages)
     Volume III: Past Performance (15 pages)
     Volume IV: Pricing
     Volume V: Required Documents

L.2  Submission via Treasury procurement portal.


SECTION M — EVALUATION FACTORS

  Factor 1 — Technical Approach              35% weight
  Factor 2 — Management Plan                 15% weight
  Factor 3 — Past Performance                30% weight
